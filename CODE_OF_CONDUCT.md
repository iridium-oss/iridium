# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

IRIDIUM is an open source project aimed at urban mobility prediction and optimization. We are committed to providing a professional and respectful environment for everyone who contributes, uses, or discusses the project. This Code of Conduct outlines expected behaviour and how to report concerns.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: the repository, issue and pull request discussions, documentation, communication channels officially associated with the project, and in-person or virtual events held under the project name. It applies to maintainers, contributors, and users alike.

## Expected Behaviour

- Be respectful and professional in all interactions.
- Welcome newcomers and help them contribute effectively.
- Focus on technical merit and constructive feedback.
- Accept that perspectives and experiences differ; engage in good faith.
- Use inclusive language and avoid behaviour that could reasonably be considered harassing, discriminatory, or offensive.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

Unacceptable behaviour includes, but is not limited to:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing or threatening to publish others' private information without consent.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or institutional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers have the right to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that do not align with this Code of Conduct. In serious or repeated cases, they may temporarily or permanently ban a participant from project spaces. Decisions will be made in line with the project governance and with the aim of preserving a professional and inclusive environment.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the project maintainers. Reports can be made via a private channel agreed by the project (e.g. a designated email or contact listed in the repository). Provide as much relevant detail as possible so that the matter can be assessed. Confidentiality will be respected to the extent consistent with resolving the situation and ensuring the safety of the community.
