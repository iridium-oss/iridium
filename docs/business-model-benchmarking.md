# Business Model Benchmarking

Market precedents that support IRIDIUM's commercial architecture are documented in [benchmark-market-patterns.md](benchmark-market-patterns.md).

That document covers:

- White-label and municipal mobility platforms (Trafi, OpenMove, O-CITY, MyTransit)
- Analytics and API monetization (Urban Sharing, MyTransit)
- On-premises and sovereign deployment (Snap4City, TwinCity3D)
- Grant and research-backed deployments (Digital Twin Cities Centre, Horizon Europe)
- Partner integrations (Trafi, Urban Sharing)
- Federated learning as an institutional differentiator in smart-city and ITS literature

Use it for strategic framing and source-linked support only. It does not list IRIDIUM partners or customers. See also [business-model.md](business-model.md) and [public-sector-commercialization.md](public-sector-commercialization.md).
