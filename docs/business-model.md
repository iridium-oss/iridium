# Business Model

This document describes IRIDIUM's commercial architecture: what can plausibly be sold, who the buyers are, and how the model aligns with real market patterns in urban mobility, MaaS, smart-city digital twin, and public-sector software. It does not state current revenue, customers, or contracts. All commercial surfaces are framed as proposed or strategic unless explicitly current.

## Separation of commercial model and traction

- **Current commercial model**: The set of revenue surfaces and offerings that the product architecture supports (platform subscriptions, analytics modules, API access, deployment options, services).
- **Current traction**: What has actually been achieved (repository maturity, demo, real-data integrations, documentation, research readiness). No invented pilots, contracts, or revenue.
- **Proposed**: Offerings that are designed and described but not yet sold or deployed.
- **Strategic**: Long-term options (research deployments, partner integrations, federated learning readiness) backed by market logic, not current commitments.

## Current monetizable architecture

The following revenue surfaces are consistent with how urban mobility and public-sector platforms are sold and deployed. Market precedent is cited; no claim is made that IRIDIUM currently sells these.

| Offering | What it is | Who pays | Market precedent |
|----------|------------|----------|-------------------|
| Core municipal mobility intelligence platform | Subscription access to digital twin, dashboards, source-backed analytics | Municipalities, transport authorities, city agencies | White-label and municipal mobility platforms (Trafi, OpenMove, O-CITY, MyTransit) |
| Operator and municipality operations dashboards | Operational visibility over network state, transit status, alerts | Transit operators, municipal mobility teams | Control-room and operations views standard in ITS |
| Planning and analytics modules | Equity, forecasting, planning with documented methodology | Planning departments, policy units | Urban Sharing Analytics API, MyTransit Transit Data API |
| API and integration access | Structured API access for integrators and institutional consumers | System integrators, city IT, partner platforms | Modular monetization of dashboards, analytics, APIs |
| Deployment and onboarding services | Implementation, configuration, go-live support | Buying institution or operator | Services revenue common in municipal technology rollout |
| Data normalization and integration services | Transit and source normalization, provider onboarding, data quality | Municipalities and operators | Standard professional services in public-sector software |
| Training and support packages | Training, change management, ongoing support | Subscribing institutions | TwinCity3D and similar offer support and training |
| White-label institutional interfaces | Institution-branded front ends and reporting | Municipalities and operators requiring own branding | Trafi, OpenMove, O-CITY, MyTransit white-label offerings |
| On-premises and sovereign deployment | Deployment in customer environment for sensitive or regulated contexts | Public-sector buyers with sovereignty or procurement requirements | Snap4City (service or on-site install); sovereign deployment standard in city tech |

## Near-term expansion (proposed)

Realistic expansion modules, not current revenue facts:

- Premium routing and journey intelligence modules
- Anomaly and disruption intelligence modules
- Equity and planning dashboards with policy-ready indicators
- Multi-operator control-room views
- Benchmarking and cross-city comparison modules
- Partner API access for integrators and third-party apps
- Institution-facing reporting exports
- Mobility-data licensing or managed data services where appropriate

## Long-term strategic optionality

Strategic pathways backed by market logic and sector trends:

- Research and grant-supported deployments (e.g. Horizon Europe mobility projects, Vinnova-funded digital twin adoption)
- Partner integrations with mobility apps and city platforms (Trafi-style and Urban Sharing-style integration paths)
- Privacy-preserving institutional AI layers
- Federated learning readiness for regulated data environments (positioned in smart-city and ITS literature as future-ready architecture)
- Regional platform expansion to other cities
- Public-sector digital infrastructure positioning

## Policy: no overclaiming

- Current commercial model and current traction are clearly separated.
- Future revenue lines are labeled as proposed or strategic.
- No invented customers, contracts, pilots, grants, or partnerships.
- No false investor-style vanity claims or fake numbers.
- No pricing stated unless the repository already contains a justified pricing framework.
