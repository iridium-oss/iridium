# Deployment Models

This document describes delivery and deployment options for IRIDIUM. Multiple models align with how public-sector and operator technology is procured and operated. Deployment choice affects procurement, data governance, sovereignty, integration with legacy systems, and institutional trust.

## Cloud-hosted subscription

- Platform delivered as a service with multi-tenant or dedicated instance.
- Standard for rapid adoption and lower initial procurement friction.
- **Who it is for**: Municipalities and operators without strict on-premises requirements.

## Municipality-hosted deployment

- Platform installed and operated in the city or agency data center.
- Data remains under direct institutional control.
- **Who it is for**: Public-sector buyers with existing IT infrastructure and data governance requirements.

## Operator-hosted deployment

- Deployment within the transit or mobility operator environment.
- Aligns with operator control over operational data.
- **Who it is for**: Transit operators and mobility service providers.

## Sovereign or on-premises deployment

- Full deployment in the customer environment for sensitive or regulated contexts.
- No data leaves the institutional boundary.
- **Who it is for**: Institutions with sovereignty, compliance, or procurement rules requiring on-premises software.
- **Market precedent**: Snap4City is offered both as a service and installed on site; TwinCity3D emphasizes open-source plus installation, support, training, and customization.

## White-label institutional deployment

- Institution-branded instance, optionally hosted by the institution or as a managed service.
- Supports co-branded or white-label positioning.
- **Who it is for**: Cities and operators requiring their own branding; consistent with Trafi, OpenMove, O-CITY white-label offerings.

## Hybrid deployment

- Sensitive data stays local; aggregated or non-sensitive workloads may use cloud.
- Supports gradual adoption and data-governance constraints.
- **Who it is for**: Institutions balancing innovation with data residency and governance.

## Why deployment choice matters

| Factor | Explanation |
|--------|-------------|
| Procurement | Public-sector procurement often specifies deployment location, data handling, and vendor obligations. Multiple deployment options support compliant tenders. |
| Data governance | Cities and operators must meet data governance and retention rules. Local or sovereign deployment supports audit and control. |
| Sovereignty | Sovereign deployment is standard in city technology stacks; it addresses institutional and regulatory expectations. |
| Legacy integration | On-premises or hybrid models ease integration with legacy systems and internal networks. |
| Institutional trust | Deployment choice supports trust: institutions can select the model that matches their risk and governance posture. |
