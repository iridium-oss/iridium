# Public-Sector Commercialization

This document explains how IRIDIUM's commercial architecture is designed for public-sector and operator adoption. It does not claim current customers or contracts. It describes what can plausibly be sold and how deployment and services align with municipal and operator procurement reality.

## Why municipalities would buy this

- **Operational visibility**: Single pane for network state, transit status, and alerts with source transparency per provider.
- **Planning support**: Equity analytics and network metrics support long-term planning; methodology is documented; no synthetic scores presented as real.
- **Source transparency**: Every figure and status can be traced to a documented source; suitable for audit and accountability.
- **Trust under partial data**: When data is partial or unavailable, the system says so; no fabricated feeds that could mislead decisions.
- **Deployment options**: Cloud, on-premises, or sovereign deployment to match procurement and data governance requirements.
- **White-label and institutional branding**: Cities can deploy under their own branding; consistent with how Trafi, OpenMove, O-CITY, and MyTransit serve municipalities.

## Why operators would buy this

- **Operations dashboards**: Control-room and operations views for transit and mobility providers.
- **Routing and journey intelligence**: Multimodal routing and planning with source-backed status (live, static, unavailable).
- **Integration with operator systems**: GTFS, real-time APIs, and data normalization as part of implementation services.
- **Operator-hosted or hybrid deployment**: Data can stay in operator environment where required.
- **Anomaly and disruption intelligence**: Detection and response path for incidents and demand surges (proposed module).

## Services layer

Implementation, integration, training, and support make the business model realistic for public-sector adoption. These are proposed as part of the commercial architecture:

- Implementation and integration support
- Provider onboarding
- Transit and source normalization work
- Operational dashboard configuration
- Training and change management
- Maintenance and support
- Data quality and provenance setup
- Research partnership support
- Custom reporting and policy-analysis support

No claim is made about current delivered engagements. Services revenue is common in municipal technology rollout; the services layer reflects this pattern.

## Deployment-led sales

Growth is driven by institutional adoption: a city or operator deploys the platform for operations or planning. This is deployment-led sales, not consumer app installs or vanity metrics. Pilot-to-platform path is framed as a credible sequence; no current pilots are invented.

## Honesty policy

- Current commercial model and current traction are clearly separated.
- Future revenue lines and expansion modules are labeled as proposed or strategic.
- No invented customers, contracts, pilots, or partnerships.
- No false investor-style claims or fake numbers.
