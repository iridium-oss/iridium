# Benchmark and Market Patterns

This document records market precedents that support IRIDIUM's commercial architecture. It is used for strategic framing only. No claim is made that IRIDIUM has the same customers, revenue, or contracts as these examples. The purpose is to show that the proposed commercial model is consistent with how urban mobility, MaaS, smart-city digital twin, and public-sector software are actually sold and deployed.

## White-label and municipal mobility platforms

- **Trafi**: White-label MaaS app, back office, journey planner, and routing configurator for cities.
- **OpenMove**: MaaS suite offered as white-label solutions.
- **O-CITY**: White-label mobile application and account-based fare platform.
- **MyTransit**: White-label and co-branded apps plus transit data APIs.

Implication: IRIDIUM's white-label institutional deployment and municipal platform subscriptions align with an established pattern.

## Analytics and API monetization

- **Urban Sharing**: Exposes an Analytics API and a MaaS API.
- **MyTransit**: Offers a Transit Data API.

Implication: A modular product strategy where dashboards, analytics, and integration APIs are separate monetizable surfaces is consistent with the market.

## On-premises and sovereign deployment

- **Snap4City**: Offered both as a service and installed in your location.
- **TwinCity3D**: Emphasizes open-source plus installation, support, training, and customization services.

Implication: IRIDIUM's on-premises or sovereign deployment option for municipalities and operators is supported by real precedent in city technology stacks.

## Grant and research-backed deployments

- **Digital Twin Cities Centre**: Publicly funded by Vinnova; focuses on municipal digital twin adoption.
- **Horizon Europe mobility projects**: Demonstrate city mobility technologies in real urban contexts.

Implication: A "research and grant-supported deployment" line is a credible strategic pathway, not an invented traction story.

## Partner integrations

- **Trafi**: Integrates with many mobility service providers.
- **Urban Sharing**: Exposes a third-party MaaS API.

Implication: Future IRIDIUM partner integrations for mobility apps, operator systems, and city platforms are a standard expansion path.

## Federated learning as institutional differentiator

- Recent smart-city and ITS literature positions federated learning as a future-ready architecture for privacy-preserving urban AI.

Implication: IRIDIUM can describe federated learning as a strategic readiness layer and institutional differentiator, not as current core revenue.
