# Literature Review

This document is a placeholder for a structured literature review supporting IRIDIUM. It will be expanded as the project progresses.

## Purpose

The literature review will cover:

1. **Federated learning for mobility and transport**: Privacy-preserving and distributed learning methods; secure aggregation; applications to traffic prediction or demand modeling.
2. **Spatio-temporal graph neural networks**: ST-GNN architectures for traffic forecasting; graph construction; temporal modeling; benchmarks and evaluation.
3. **Multimodal routing and optimization**: Multi-objective routing (time, cost, carbon); transfer modeling; real-time and adaptive routing.
4. **Mobility equity and accessibility**: Indicators of accessibility and equity; spatial analysis; limitations and bias in transport data and metrics.
5. **Anomaly detection in transport**: Incident detection; event-driven demand; methods and operational considerations.
6. **Digital twins for cities**: Conceptual and technical literature on urban digital twins; data integration and update mechanisms.

## Structure (Planned)

Sections will be added for each theme above, with subsections for key papers, methods, and relevance to IRIDIUM. Citations will follow a consistent format (e.g. author-year or numbered references). Links to open-access versions or DOIs will be included where possible.

This document is currently a placeholder. Contributions that add summaries of relevant work, with clear relevance to the project's architecture or methodology, are welcome. New sections should use formal, academic language and avoid unsupported claims.
